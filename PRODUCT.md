## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: decision-makers at B2B aviation organizations — MRO shops, Part 135 charter operators, FBOs — who need modern marketing and sales infrastructure (customer intelligence, CRM/lead scoring, paid media, AI-driven systems) but have had no partner who understands both advanced marketing operations and aviation's relationship-driven, nuance-heavy reality.

Secondary: private aviation individuals needing a trusted concierge advisor. This is an adjacent, smaller line of business — B2B is the primary focus.

## Product Purpose

Wingspan brings the marketing practices, tooling, and strategic rigor used by the most sophisticated tech companies (Google, Salesforce, HubSpot-caliber customer intelligence, predictive lead scoring, always-on paid media, AI-driven sales systems) to aviation businesses, deployed inside aviation's specific operating reality. Success means a client's marketing/sales function reaches that same standard of sophistication without losing aviation-specific fluency.

## Positioning

The differentiating mechanism is the combination, not either half alone: big-tech-grade marketing operations expertise paired with genuine, hands-on aviation-industry relationships and experience (built across Aman Resorts, Volato, Jet Aviation, and Admiral Technologies). A generalist marketing agency would lack the aviation fluency; an aviation-industry insider would lack the advanced marketing systems. Confirmed as the intended positioning.
